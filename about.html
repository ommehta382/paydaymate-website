Commission shouldbe the easy part.

Payday Mate is a two-person team in Sydney building commission software for Australian sales teams.

Why we're building it

Every rep knows the feeling: you close the deal, then wait weeks to find out what it was worth.

Behind that wait is usually a spreadsheet only one person understands, a manager answering the same questions every month, and a payroll team keying numbers in by hand. Since Payday Super, that same spreadsheet now decides whether super lands on time.

The big commission tools are built for large companies and priced to match. We're building Payday Mate for the teams of 5 to 50 they leave behind, with Australian payroll and Payday Super built in from day one.
